What It Does

Four AI-powered capabilities delivered from a single PDF upload.

📄

PDF Upload

Upload any research paper as a PDF. Title, abstract, and full text are extracted automatically using PyMuPDF.

🧠

AI Classification

Fine-tuned SciBERT models categorize papers across 8 main research domains and 40 subcategories aligned with arXiv taxonomy.

✍️

Smart Summary

Google Gemini generates a focused academic summary covering the paper’s objective, methodology, and key findings.

🔍

Paper Recommendations

Queries the live arXiv API for 20 candidates, then re-ranks them by semantic cosine similarity to surface the top 10.

How It Works

Three steps from raw PDF to actionable insights.

1

Upload Your Paper

Select any research PDF. The system extracts text, title, and abstract — no manual input required.

2

AI Pipeline Runs

SciBERT classifies the topic, YAKE pulls the top keywords, and Gemini produces a concise academic summary.

3

Discover & Save

Browse live arXiv recommendations ranked by relevance. Save results to your personal private library.
